# Campus preparedness

*Idea / RUHealthHack exploration* · Projects / The workbench

> What happens when a campus has to respond to an outbreak?

An idea for simulating dorms, classes, gatherings, staff shortages, and supplies, then comparing intervention strategies over time.

**My part.** Problem research and hackathon ideation.

**Outcome.** Compare strategies before a crisis. A concept under exploration. Nothing has been built yet.

**Stack.** Simulation, Public health, Scenario design

**Workflow.** Model the campus → Simulate spread → Compare interventions

## Technical detail and evidence

- Scenarios considered: ventilation and communication strategies, supply constraints, and access to testing, vaccines, and treatment.

**Connected to.** Nomos, Penn Medicine
