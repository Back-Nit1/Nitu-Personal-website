# Outreach engine

*Experiment* · Projects / The workbench

> Researching the people worth reaching out to.

A personal automation for researching professors, mentors, startups, and clubs at a scale of 50 to 100+ targets. Research and email drafting are separate agents, so one can’t paper over the other.

**My part.** Structured research, caching, and streaming JSON workflows.

**Outcome.** Research and drafting as separate stages. An unfinished experiment.

**Stack.** Agents, Structured JSON, Caching

**Workflow.** Research agent → Cached JSON → Email agent

**Connected to.** Campus leadership, Praedos, Kairos
